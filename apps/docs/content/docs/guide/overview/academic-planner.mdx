## Overview

The **Academic Planner** shows the structure of your teaching programme as a
hierarchy from academic level down to subject and class. It helps you plan what
you teach and spot gaps before you schedule.

## What you'll see

- A tree of levels, subjects and classes.
- Counts of active items per level and subject.
- Warnings for items that are set up but not yet used.

![the academic planner hierarchy tree](/images/guide/overview-academic-planner-main.png)

## Common tasks

### Review your academic structure

1. Open **Academic Planner**.
2. Expand a level to see its subjects.
3. Expand a subject to see its classes.
4. Check for anything unused or missing before scheduling.

## Notes

- Levels, subjects and classes are each managed in their own screen under
  [Centre Setup](/docs/guide/centre-setup/subjects).
- The planner is read-oriented; use the dedicated modules to add or edit items.
