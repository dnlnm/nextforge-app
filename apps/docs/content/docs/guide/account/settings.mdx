## Overview

**Settings** is where you configure the centre's details and manage your plan.
You control the centre name, logo and currency shown across the product.

## What you'll see

- Centre details (name, logo, branding).
- Currency setting.
- Links to subscription and billing.

![the centre settings page](/images/guide/account-settings-main.png)

![the subscription / billing screen](/images/guide/account-settings-subscription.png)

## Common tasks

### Update centre details

1. Open **Settings**.
2. Update the centre name or logo.
3. Choose the display currency.
4. Save.

### Review your plan and billing

1. Open **Settings**.
2. Go to the subscription screen.
3. Review your current plan and billing details.

## Notes

- Currency is applied across the centre's money displays in MYR by default.
- Subscription is billed separately (via Stripe); it is not part of tuition
  payment recording.
