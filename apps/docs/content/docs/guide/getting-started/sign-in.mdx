## Overview

Sign in to access your centre. KLIO.MY uses a single account; after signing in
you land on the dashboard of your active centre.

## What you'll see

- An email and password form.
- A "forgot password" link if you cannot sign in.
- A sign-up link if you do not have an account yet.

![the full sign-in card](/images/guide/getting-started-sign-in-main.png)

## Common tasks

### Sign in

1. Open the KLIO.MY sign-in page.
2. Enter your email address and password.
3. Select **Sign in**.
4. You are taken to your centre dashboard.

### Reset your password

1. On the sign-in screen, select **Forgot password**.
2. Enter your email address.
3. Follow the link sent to your inbox to set a new password.

## Notes

- Your username is separate from your account email. You can change both later
  from your account settings.
- If you belong to more than one centre you can switch centres after signing in —
  see [Working with Centres](/docs/guide/getting-started/centres).
