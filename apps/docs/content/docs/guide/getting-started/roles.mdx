## Overview

KLIO.MY gives each member a role that controls what they can see and do. Knowing
the roles helps you decide who to invite and what each person can manage.

## The roles

- **Owner** — full control of everything, including members, settings and
  subscription.
- **Admin** — runs day-to-day records, schedules, billing and reporting. An admin
  sees everything except the Members screen.
- **Teacher** — a focused view of today's classes and attendance only.

## What each role can do

| Area | Owner | Admin | Teacher |
|------|------:|------:|--------:|
| Dashboard, Today, Schedules | Yes | Yes | Today only |
| Students, Classes, Attendance | Yes | Yes | Attendance only |
| Invoices, Payments, Reports | Yes | Yes | No |
| Members, Settings, Subscription | Yes | No | No |

## Common tasks

### Choose a role for a new member

1. Open **Members**.
2. Select **Invite member**.
3. Choose **Owner**, **Admin** or **Teacher**.
4. Send the invitation. The member accepts it to join.

## Notes

- The Owner role includes everything an Admin can do.
- Teacher accounts are intentionally narrow, so keep the Teacher role for people
  who only need to mark attendance and see today's classes.
