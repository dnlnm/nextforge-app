## Overview

**Subjects** is where you list what the centre teaches, such as Mathematics,
English or Science. Subjects are the building blocks for classes.

## What you'll see

- A list of subjects.
- Each subject's status (active or archived).
- A way to add, edit or manage subjects.

![the subjects list](/images/guide/centre-setup-subjects-main.png)

## Common tasks

### Add a subject

1. Open **Subjects**.
2. Select **Add subject**.
3. Enter the subject name.
4. Confirm.

### Archive a subject

1. Open **Subjects**.
2. Select the subject.
3. Choose to archive it. It is retired without deleting its history.

## Notes

- A subject can be linked to several classes at different levels. See
  [Academic Planner](/docs/guide/overview/academic-planner).
- Archiving keeps existing records intact while removing the subject from new
  setups.
