## Overview

**Teachers** keeps the records of the people who teach at your centre. Assign a
teacher to classes so schedules and attendance are attributed correctly.

## What you'll see

- A list of teachers.
- **Add teacher** and invite actions.
- A detail view for each teacher.

![the teachers list](/images/guide/centre-setup-teachers-main.png)

## Common tasks

### Add a teacher

1. Open **Teachers**.
2. Select **Add teacher**.
3. Enter the teacher's details.
4. Confirm. The teacher is added and can be assigned to classes.

### Invite a teacher to sign in

1. Open **Teachers**.
2. Select **Invite**.
3. Send the invitation. The teacher accepts to join the centre.

## Notes

- Teachers who sign in get the Teacher role, which is limited to Today and
  Attendance. See [Roles & Workspaces](/docs/guide/getting-started/roles).
