## Overview

**Members** is centre access. As an Owner you control who can sign in to the
centre and what they can do through their role.

## What you'll see

- A list of current members and their roles.
- A **pending invitations** section for members who have not accepted yet.
- An **Invite member** button.

![the members list with pending invitations](/images/guide/centre-setup-members-main.png)

![the invite-member dialog](/images/guide/centre-setup-members-dialog.png)

## Common tasks

### Invite a member

1. Open **Members**.
2. Select **Invite member**.
3. Enter the member's details and choose a role.
4. Send the invitation.

### Remove a member

1. Open **Members**.
2. Select the member.
3. Choose **Remove**. Their access is revoked.

## Notes

- Only the Owner sees and manages Members; Admins and Teachers do not. See
  [Roles & Workspaces](/docs/guide/getting-started/roles).
- Removing a member does not delete the records they created.
