## Overview

**Students** holds the records of everyone enrolled at your centre. This is where
you add new students, keep their details up to date, and archive or remove
records you no longer need.

## What you'll see

- A searchable table with filters.
- KPI chips showing active counts and outstanding amounts.
- **Add student** and **Import** buttons.
- Row actions to view, archive or delete a student.
- A detail view for each student (contact, level, classes, fees).

## Common tasks

### Add a single student

1. Open **Students**.
2. Select **Add student**.
3. Enter the student's details and guardian contact.
4. Confirm. The student is saved to your centre.

### Import many students

1. Open **Students**.
2. Select **Import**.
3. Upload your spreadsheet.
4. Review the validation results and fix any issues.
5. Confirm the import. The students are added.

### Archive a student

1. Find the student.
2. Select the archive action.
3. Confirm. The student leaves the active list but the record is kept.

## Notes

- Student photos and documents are stored privately.
- Use sample data when capturing screenshots — do not expose real student or
  guardian information.
