## About this guide

This guide is written for the owners and administrators of a tuition centre. It
walks through each screen in KLIO.MY and explains what it does and how to use it
day to day.

The pages are grouped the same way as the sidebar:

- **Get Started** — signing in, working with centres, and how roles work.
- **Overview** — the dashboard and the daily planning screens.
- **Centre Setup** — the people, classes, subjects and rooms you manage.
- **Operations** — attendance, invoicing, payments and reports.
- **Account** — centre settings and billing.

## Before you begin

You need an active KLIO.MY account and at least one centre. See
[Sign In](/docs/guide/getting-started/sign-in) to get started.
