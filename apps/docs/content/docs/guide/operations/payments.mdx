## Overview

**Payments** records money received against invoices. KLIO.MY records payments
that come in through external methods — you note them here so balances stay up to
date.

## What you'll see

- A list of recorded payments with method and amount.
- Outstanding totals.
- A **Record payment** button.
- A detail view and receipt for each payment.

![the payments list](/images/guide/operations-payments-main.png)

![the record-payment form](/images/guide/operations-payments-dialog.png)

## Common tasks

### Record a payment

1. Open **Payments**.
2. Select **Record payment**.
3. Choose the student or invoice, the amount and the date.
4. Pick the payment method (Cash, Bank Transfer, DuitNow, FPX, Card, Other).
5. Confirm. The amount is applied to the invoice.

### Check outstanding balance

1. Open **Payments**.
2. Read the outstanding totals.
3. Follow up on any unpaid invoices.

## Notes

- KLIO.MY does not process guardian payments directly; it records payments already
  received through your other channels.
- Amounts are stored as integer sen and shown in MYR.
