## Overview

**Invoices** is where monthly fees are generated and tracked. You create invoices
for a billing month, then watch their status as they are paid.

## What you'll see

- A list of invoices with status badges (Issued, Partially Paid, Overdue).
- Totals and outstanding amounts.
- A **Generate invoices** action for a chosen billing month.
- A detail view for each invoice.

![the invoices list with status badges](/images/guide/operations-invoices-main.png)

![the generate-invoices dialog](/images/guide/operations-invoices-dialog.png)

## Common tasks

### Generate monthly invoices

1. Open **Invoices**.
2. Select **Generate invoices**.
3. Choose the billing month.
4. Confirm. Invoices are created for the month's enrollments.

### Review which invoices are unpaid

1. Open **Invoices**.
2. Filter by status.
3. Review any **Overdue** or **Partially Paid** invoices.

## Notes

- Amounts are shown in MYR and are based on each student's applied monthly fee.
- Invoices move to **Paid** when payments are recorded — see
  [Payments](/docs/guide/operations/payments).
